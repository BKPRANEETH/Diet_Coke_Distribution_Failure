# References & Data Provenance

**Important:** The macro facts below are real and cited. The order-line dataset in `data/raw/` is a **simulation** calibrated to these sources (see `docs/data_dictionary.md`), not real Coca-Cola data.

## The April 2026 Diet Coke shortage (event, cause, geography)
1. Business Standard — *"Why are Diet Coke cans unavailable and what's behind the supply crunch?"* (21 Apr 2026). Shortage across Indian cities incl. Mumbai; packaging-shortage + demand-surge "perfect storm"; BAI 120–130 m can-unit shortfall in 2025.
2. Outlook Business — *"West Asia War Hits India's Summer Sips: Diet Coke Shortage Spreads Across Major Cities"* (23 Apr 2026). Strait-of-Hormuz blockade; ~9% of global aluminium from the Gulf (DAM Capital); two Coca-Cola distributors told Reuters the company was rationing / not fulfilling some orders.
3. Open Magazine — *"Where did all the Diet Coke go? India's most unexpected 2026 shortage explained"* (21 Apr 2026). Bare shelves in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Ahmedabad; Coca-Cola acknowledgment re at-home consumption + aluminium/ingredient shortages; availability-heuristic panic.
4. Packaging South Asia — *"Packaging dependence exposes India Diet Coke shortage amid aluminium supply crunch"* (30 Apr 2026). Diet Coke predominantly sold in cans, minimal PET/glass; Coca-Cola India sales ~US$533 m FY25 (Reuters); "unprecedented in recent decades" aluminium disruption.
5. Sahi.com — *"Diet Coke Shortage India 2026: The Can Crisis Explained"* (29 Apr 2026). Shelves bare since mid-April in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Gurugram, Pune; Blinkit cap of 4 cans/customer; low-sugar 5% (2020) → 30% (2025) of Coca-Cola India volumes; Diet Coke ~2× YoY; zero-sugar segment ~$4.7 bn by 2030; Mar-2025 ₹10 zero-sugar launches.

## OTIF benchmarks
6. FourKites — *Maximizing On-Time-In-Full (OTIF) in the Supply Chain.* FMCG/CPG OTIF target high-80s–90s%.
7. MetricHQ — *On-Time In-Full (OTIF).* Retail & CPG grocery 96–99%; multiplication method (OT% × IF%); immature orgs often discover true OTIF 15–20% below belief.
8. TransVirtual / Kaizen Institute — OTIF "good" band 85–95%, excellent 95–98%, <85% systemic; Walmart 98% with 3% COGS penalty.

## Unit-economics anchor (FMCG beverage in India)
9. Varun Beverages disclosures (Business Standard, MarketScreener, 2022–2024) — net realization ≈ ₹164/case (mixed CSD/NCB portfolio) used as a sanity anchor; per-case economics for premium can SKUs scaled above this (documented in data dictionary).
